Privacy Policy

BUGUN ADS · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what data the BUGUN ADS application ("the app", "we") collects, why, how long we keep it, who can see it, and how you can have it deleted.

1. Who we are

The app is operated by BUGUN MEDIA, Qarshi, Qashqadaryo region, Republic of Uzbekistan. Contact for any privacy question: elbeshmurodov@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

The app connects to Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad accounts that you manage, reads campaign structure and performance statistics, shows reports, and proposes optimization recommendations. Actions such as pausing an ad or changing a budget are executed only after an authorized person approves them, and every change is logged.

3. Data we collect

Account data you provide: name, email address, password hash (never the password itself), interface language.

Data received from Meta through Facebook Login and the Marketing API, only after you grant permission:

  • your Facebook user ID and the list of permissions you granted;
  • ad account ID, name, status, currency, time zone, business portfolio name;
  • names, statuses, objectives, budgets and targeting settings of campaigns, ad sets and ads;
  • performance statistics: spend, impressions, reach, frequency, clicks, results and cost per result;
  • names and IDs of Facebook Pages linked to your ad accounts.

We do not access your personal Facebook profile content, friend list or private messages.

Access tokens issued by Meta are stored encrypted in our database and are decrypted only at the moment a request is sent to Meta. They are never written to logs.

Technical data: server logs with request time, page address, browser type and IP address, used only for security and troubleshooting.

4. Why we use the data

  • to show you reports about your advertising;
  • to generate optimization recommendations;
  • to apply changes you approve to your ads;
  • to send you notifications (for example via Telegram) about results and alerts;
  • to keep an audit log of who changed what.

We do not sell data, do not use it to advertise third-party products to you, and do not build profiles of individuals.

6. Data retention

  • Account data, advertising statistics and audit logs are kept while your account exists.
  • Meta access tokens are deleted immediately when you disconnect the app or remove it in your Facebook settings.
  • Server logs are rotated automatically.
  • After a deletion request, all your data is deleted within 30 days.

7. Who can see your data

Data of each connected account is visible only to users you authorize in the app. Our staff access it only to resolve a support request you make.

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to show, correct, export or delete your data, and you can revoke the app's access at any time in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations. Instructions: Data Deletion.

9. Children

The app is intended for businesses and is not directed to persons under 18.

10. Changes

We will update the date at the top of this page when the policy changes and notify active users by email about significant changes.
