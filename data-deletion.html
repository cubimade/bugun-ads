Data Deletion Instructions

BUGUN ADS

You can have all data the BUGUN ADS app holds about you and your connected ad accounts deleted in either of two ways:

Option 1 — Remove the app in Facebook

  1. Open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings.
  2. Go to Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  3. Find bugun-ads and click Remove.

Your access token stops working immediately and is deleted from our database.

Option 2 — Email request

Send an email to elbeshmurodov@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request – BUGUN ADS" and the Facebook name or ad account ID you connected.

What happens next

All your data — account details, stored advertising statistics, recommendations and audit records — is permanently deleted within 30 days, and we confirm by email.
