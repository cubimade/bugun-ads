by BUGUN MEDIA · Qarshi, Uzbekistan

BUGUN ADS is an advertising analytics and management tool operated by BUGUN MEDIA, a digital marketing agency. It connects to Meta ad accounts that the user manages, shows campaign performance, and suggests optimizations (for example, pausing ads with no results or adjusting budgets). Changes to ads are made only after a person approves them.
